# M-20261014-09

- **Evidence:** `dispatch.sh` appends each TASK line, with its read marker, to the record's JSON block under `dispatch` (the dispatch evidence step 6 and the owner tasks' item 1 cite); the chain's `record-block.py` then writes the gate block (`format`, `task`, `head`, `pr`, `ledger`, `searches`, `audit_findings`) from scratch, and the gate rejects unknown keys, so the `dispatch` list vanished from every record whose chain ran: a05, a06, T704, the hook task and the playbook owner task. The hook record's prose says "the line in the dispatch list above" four times over an empty block. Restored at 07:46Z from messages.db (the bus is the source of truth) into a `## Dispatch (bus lines …)` section of each record.
- **Detection and why not earlier:** three merges (a05/a06 as #350, T704 as #352, the hook as #353) passed their audits with the list already gone; the auditors read the prose and the diff, and only the #354 audit checked the cited list. The orchestrator never re-read a record's block after a chain run.
- **Root cause:** two writers of one JSON block with two grammars (dispatch.sh's `dispatch`, the gate's closed key set), and a block writer that replaces instead of merging.
- **Should have been:** the dispatch lines live where the gate's grammar never touches them (a prose section, or a `dispatch` key the gate's grammar allows), and the block writer preserves whatever it does not own.
