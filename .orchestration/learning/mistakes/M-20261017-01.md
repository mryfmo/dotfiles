# M-20261017-01

- **Evidence:** the bus lines of 21:43:54Z and 21:50:18Z (`acc-cgs2-unblock.txt`, `acc-cgs2-unblock2.txt`: the untracked evidence copies in worker-e blocked `git switch -c`; the answer was to remove them and continue), and `scripts/regime-counters.sh dotfiles-T128-chain-github-scripts-followup-a02 --pr 375` reading `revises 2`.
- **Detection and why not earlier:** the ACCEPTANCE line was the only channel the Stop gate accepts for an unanswered RESULT, and `revise` was the nearest status to "do something and continue"; the counter's reading was not checked at send time.
- **Root cause:** INV-6 counts `status=revise` lines as revisions of the work, so an answer that revises nothing but travels as `revise` inflates S2; the regime has a non-counting status for answers (`pending-integration`, used the same day for T13's and T15's questions) and the orchestrator did not use it here.
- **Should have been:** the two unblock answers as `status=pending-integration` with `next_action=git-clean,fresh-branch,task`, so INV-6 reads the real revise count (0 before the first real round).
- **Corrections:** C-56.
