# U121 brief-check: a brief names its closing form and its trust boundary before round 1 (decision 2026-10-30 change 4, plan D1 and spec R1, standing order 37)

GOAL
Every one of the 13 treadmill rows in handoff/02-audit-treadmill-analysis.md and today's U109 (24 rounds) and U112 (18 rounds) began with a brief that asked for a hand-written check over an open-ended input (shell text, workflow text, git state, a process tree, a path shape, a producer's output) and named no closing form; the form (an AST walk, one strict parser module, an exact allowlist, removal) was found after three or more rounds. The research measured why a denylist over such inputs never closes (research/04 entries 14 and 15; research/07 section 3). U109 also showed that the auditor judges outside the unit's threat boundary unless the body states it (rounds 21 to 24). This unit is spec R1 of handoff/03-remediation-spec.md plus a TRUST section, as a script that refuses a brief, so no TASK is sent for a brief that will treadmill.

CHANGE
1. Data shape first: a brief is a sequence of upper-case section headings (`GOAL`, `SCOPE`, `CHANGE`, `CONTEXT`, `ACCEPTANCE`, `TIMEBOX`, `FORBIDDEN`, `CLOSURE`, `TRUST`, `REPORT`, `STANDING`, plus `WORKER RULES (...)` lines) each followed by its text; model it as one parser that returns `dict[str, str]` and refuses a duplicate heading. The trigger terms are one tuple with a one-line reason each (take R1's table and add: `bound`, `refuse`, `fail closed`, `untrusted`, `sandbox`, `lint`, `rule`, `allowlist`, `denylist`, `parse`, `tokeniz`, `regex`, `walk`, `census`); the closing forms are one tuple of four (`ast`, `parser-module`, `allowlist`, `remove`).
2. `brief-check.py <brief>`: exit 2 on usage or an unreadable file; exit 1 with one reason per line when (a) GOAL, CHANGE or CONTEXT holds a trigger term and there is no `CLOSURE` section, or it names zero or two or more forms, or names a word outside the four; (b) a trigger term is present and there is no `TRUST` section, or it is under 40 characters, or it does not contain the word `trusted` or `trusts`; (c) CLOSURE names `allowlist` or `ast` and CHANGE contains `denylist` or `forbidden words` or `list of commands` (a denylist dressed as a form). Exit 0 otherwise, printing nothing. Matching is case-insensitive on word boundaries; a trigger term inside a backtick span is still a trigger (the brief talks about the thing either way).
3. SKILL.md step 1 gains the sentence: `Run scripts/orchestrator/brief-check.py briefs/U<n>-<slug>.md and send no TASK while it exits non-zero.` README row.
4. Tests: R1's list (each trigger term alone exits 1 without CLOSURE; each form exits 0; two forms or an unknown word exits 1; no trigger and no CLOSURE exits 0; unreadable exits 2) plus: TRUST missing exits 1; TRUST too short exits 1; the denylist-dressed-as-allowlist case exits 1; a duplicate heading exits 2; the script run over every brief under the live store (read-only, `for f in briefs/*.md`) with the exit codes pasted whole in the body's Verification (expected: most historical briefs exit 1; that is the measurement, not a failure of this unit; do not edit the store).
