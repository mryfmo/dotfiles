DATA SHAPE
One record per connection at `<seats-root>/.state/<router seat>/broker/<id>`, `<id>` the slot name `acp-<name>-<stamp>-<pid>`, written by the reaper alone (`printf` to `<id>.tmp`, then `mv -f`), never by the handler: its first form, present before omp starts, is two lines `ref=refs/acp-seat/<name>/<stamp>-<pid>` and `reaper=<pid>`; its final form adds one line `status=<omp's status, or 2 when the slot remains> push=<ref|none|failed|skipped>`. The reaper closes its copy of the connection's stdout only after that `mv`, so the socket's close is the signal that the record is complete. One reaper per connection: a subshell forked before omp, outside omp's process group, with `trap '' HUP INT TERM`, holding the connection's stdout and reading one control pipe from the handler; the handler writes `done <status> <push word>` after `push_back`, or nothing (its death closes the pipe). On that line or on EOF the reaper ends omp's group (TERM, a bounded grace polled with `kill -0`, then KILL), writes the exit line to the launch log, writes the record's final line and runs `slot_remove`. The stream carries exactly one broker line, the first, before omp starts: `acp-broker: refused: <reason>` or `acp-broker: record <id>`; every later byte is the worker's and reaches the client's stdout unread. The client, after `recv` returns empty, reads the record from the socket directory (resolving `<id>` from the first line alone), prints `acp-seat: push=<word>` on stderr, unlinks the record and exits with `status`; a missing or incomplete record is exit 2 naming `<id>`, the stream already written out. Deleted: the trailer `printf`, `TRAILER`, `pending`/`release`, `connection_signal`, every `trap` on HUP INT TERM in a connection, `exit_line`'s trap caller.
