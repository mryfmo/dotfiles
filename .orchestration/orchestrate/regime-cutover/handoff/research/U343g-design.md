## 1. The premise rounds 3 and 4 shared

The connection is one byte stream that carries both the worker's output and the broker's exit report: omp's stdout is the handler's stdout (acp-broker:1322-1323, :1326), the trailer is one more `printf` on it (acp-broker:1347), and the client picks the trailer out of that stream by a regex on its last complete line (acp-seat:44, :190, :218-223). The connection's teardown is a bash signal trap (acp-broker:1256, :1228-1250) that races the worker, the push and a recursive removal in the same process, with the trap dropped before the final removal (acp-broker:1339, :1343).

## 3. Recommendation

Channel (a) with the reaper teardown. It beats (b) because no descriptor crosses the sandbox boundary and no launch line can leak the report to the worker; it beats (c) because the wire stays the raw ACP stream and acp-relay.py stays untouched, as every U343 brief has kept it. It is the only pair in which the exit report and the teardown have one writer, the reaper, which runs when the handler is already dead and cannot be reached by the signals the relay sends; the record names the ref before the push begins, so the client never learns a ref by the outcome of a race, which is the class the round-3 P2 :1284 and the round-4 P1 :1347 both belong to.

DATA SHAPE. One record per connection at `<seats-root>/.state/<router seat>/broker/<id>`, `<id>` the slot name `acp-<name>-<stamp>-<pid>`, written by the reaper alone by `printf` to `<id>.tmp` and `mv -f`, never by the handler and never by the trailer; its first form, present before omp starts, is `ref=refs/acp-seat/<name>/<stamp>-<pid>` and `reaper=<pid>`; its final form adds one line `status=<omp's status, or 2 when the slot remains> push=<ref|none|failed|skipped>`, and the reaper closes its stdout only after that `mv`, so the socket's close is the signal that the record is complete. The stream carries exactly one broker line, the first, `acp-broker: refused: <reason>` or `acp-broker: record <id>`; every later byte is the worker's and is written to the client's stdout unread. The client exits with `status` after reading the record and unlinks it; a missing or incomplete record is exit 2 naming `<id>`, with the stream already written out. The launch log keeps its three lines (acp-broker:80-83), the exit line written by the reaper. No trailer, no `TRAILER` regex, no `connection_signal`, no `trap` on HUP, INT or TERM in a connection.

## 4. The closing form word

`remove`. The trailer rule and the signal trap are not narrowed or guarded, they cease to exist: the `printf` at acp-broker:1347, the `TRAILER` regex and the `pending`/`release` logic at acp-seat:44 and :171-194, `connection_signal` at acp-broker:1228-1250 and the arming at :1256 and :1339 all go. Nothing is parsed from the stream (not `parser-module`, not `ast`), no list of accepted shapes is kept (not `allowlist`), and the group-escape and reaper-KILL cases are threat-table residual rows, not the closing form of the row.
