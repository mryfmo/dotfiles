Status: `ready_for_review`

`make doctor REPAIR=1` now performs one least-disruptive repair round over findings produced by the normal runtime scan, then re-scans to prove convergence. With REPAIR unset, the existing stdout/stderr/exit behavior is unchanged and no repair function is called.

## Repair mapping

- Source-derived missing file or directory: `chezmoi apply <single-target>`.
- Content/managed-key drift: `chezmoi apply <single-target>`.
- Missing executable bit: `chmod +x <target>`.
- A manifest-present asset step with one or more missing recorded paths: source `scripts/update-agent-assets.sh` in a fresh strict Bash process and invoke only the whitelisted recorded step function.
- T58 STALE and ORPHAN warnings: report/suggest only; never pass them to an action function and never remove them.

Successful commands print exactly one `repaired: <category> <target> (<action>)` line. After all first-scan actions, doctor performs one re-scan. Any remaining repairable finding prints `non-convergent after repair` and exits nonzero; there is no repair loop.

## Asset ruling and source safety

The orchestrator ruled via AGMSG that repairable asset drift is only a step already present in the T57 manifest whose recorded paths are partially or fully missing. Three exclusions are pinned:

1. Steps absent from the manifest are not drift; doctor must not install skipped optional assets.
2. `source_version` mismatch is not repair drift; upgrades remain `make update`'s job and reporting remains T56's domain.
3. Unknown/ambiguous step identities are never invoked.

The updater itself was not changed. Its existing `BASH_SOURCE[0] == $0` main guard makes sourcing side-effect-free. The repair subprocess sources it under its existing `set -Eeuo pipefail`, prepends the normal mise shim path, and invokes a fixed whitelist without `eval`. The argument-bearing `ensure_mise_npm_agent_cli` step reconstructs exactly one Claude or Codex identity only from the fixed recorded install-command suffix; ambiguous identities are rejected. A strict-mode fixture proves `main` and sibling steps are not called.
