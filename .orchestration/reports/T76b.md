# T76b report: registration grammar symmetry

## Result

[memory:decision] The exact agmsg identifier grammar now lives in one shared
`lib/identifier.sh` helper and is enforced before mutation by `send.sh`,
`join.sh`, `rename.sh`, and `rename-team.sh`. A team or identity that can be
created or renamed can therefore also use the message bus.

## Boundary inventory

The complete create/rename inventory under the agmsg scripts tree is:

- `join.sh`: creates a team directory/config and creates or extends an agent
  registration.
- `rename.sh`: renames an identity in team config and message rows.
- `rename-team.sh`: renames a team directory/config and message rows.

`leave.sh` and `reset.sh` only delete registrations; `config.sh` and
`delivery.sh` modify non-registration settings. No other script creates or
renames a team or identity.
